Life expectancy

Each future year’s benefit is weighted by the probability you’re alive to receive it, from the SSA period life table (ssa.gov) — cross-checked against the CDC/NCHS US life tables. The ranked figures are the resulting expected values; the year-by-year shows one representative lifetime (each person to their estimated age) so it reads as real income.

Simplifications: it is a period table (today’s mortality, not a cohort projection), so it is slightly conservative on longevity{% if is_couple %}; each spouse’s lifespan is treated as independent, and the survivor is assumed to keep the deceased’s claim-age benefit{% endif %}. The “shorter / longer” choice shifts the estimate by a few years for someone who expects to differ from average.
