Total is the sum of every year’s benefit as paid (grown by the SS adjustment). Present value (PV) restates that in today’s dollars by discounting each future year at inflation, so money far in the future counts for what it is worth now.

The Social Security cost-of-living adjustment (the “SS adjustment” above) is modeled at about {{ cola_lag_pct }} below inflation, its long-run pattern — so benefits keep most, but not quite all, of their purchasing power over time. Set the inflation rate to match how fast you expect prices to rise; the adjustment follows from it.
