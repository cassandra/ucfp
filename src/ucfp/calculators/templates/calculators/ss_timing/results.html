Total is the sum of every year’s benefit as paid (grown by the SS adjustment). Present value (PV) restates that in today’s dollars, {% if is_opportunity_cost %}discounting each future year at your expected asset return ({{ real_return_pct }} above inflation): a deferred benefit is weighed against the growth that money could otherwise earn, the opportunity cost of waiting{% else %}discounting each future year at inflation, so money far in the future counts for what it is worth now{% endif %}.

The Social Security cost-of-living adjustment (the “SS adjustment” above) is modeled at about {{ cola_lag_pct }} below inflation, its long-run pattern — so benefits keep most, but not quite all, of their purchasing power over time. Set the inflation rate to match how fast you expect prices to rise; the adjustment follows from it.
