{% comment %} The Books of Account drill-down table for a run, rendered as a single #books-table element so a column operation can swap it in place (antinode replace_map). Rows are the run's periods, led by an opening row (the starting position before the first period). Columns come from the user's lens: a shown column reads a balance at period end (assets at market value) for asset/liability/equity/Net Worth, or a flow over the period for revenue/expense, and carries the structural controls. A summary is the header of its group: expand reveals its members beside it, collapse folds them back; removing a summary folds its group into one thin sliver. A removed column keeps its place as a sliver with a "+" to show it again. Each header is tinted by its top-level group and drill depth (see css/books_table.css).
