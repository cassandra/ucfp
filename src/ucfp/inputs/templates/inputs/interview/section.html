A section may instead supply a custom template: an `inner_template` renders inside the Next form (custom-laid-out inputs), while an `outer_template` renders outside it (a self-saving pane that manages its own inner forms, e.g. external factors or the spending drill -- nesting a form inside the Next form would be invalid HTML). The Next button only advances the flow; an outer_template pane persists its own edits. Form-level (non-field) errors are shown for a hand-laid inner_template, which otherwise renders no error feedback; form.as_p renders its own, and an outer_template's data is saved by its own inner forms, so neither needs the shared block here. {% endcomment %}
