{% comment %} A raw money input -- a Bootstrap input-group with a leading "$" and the js-money grouping hook -- for hand-rendered rows where a Django MoneyField BoundField is not available (e.g. a rowset's cloned rows). Mirrors the MoneyInput widget (django/forms/widgets/money.html); the affix markup is small enough to repeat here rather than force a BoundField. The form parses the posted value through a MoneyField, so grouping and the $ are display-only. Params: name, value, label (the screen-reader label, default "Amount"). {% endcomment %}
