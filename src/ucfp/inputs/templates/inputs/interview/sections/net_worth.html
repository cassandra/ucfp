Adjust net worth toward what you could actually keep after tax. Leave both at 0% to report gross balances (the default). Setting a rate books an estimate of the tax embedded in your accounts — the ordinary rate on pre-tax retirement balances (taxable in full when withdrawn) and the capital-gains rate on unrealized investment gains — as a liability, so net worth reflects the realizable amount. It is an estimate, not a forecast of the actual tax you will pay; a primary residence is excluded.
