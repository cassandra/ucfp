{% comment %} The add/edit editor for one net-new future vehicle, rendered as an input-editor-card into the Vehicle plan's shared editor slot (id=vehicle-editor) beside the list. Its header names the vehicle (from `heading`, or "New vehicle" when just added) with the same "Future" badge its list card carries. It auto-saves in the background (js-autosave) and always edits a known handle (add mints one), so a new vehicle has a stable identity from the first keystroke. Two groups: the vehicle (what it is, when, how often, and its price) and payment (how it is paid). The payment-method radio drives a switch (inputs.js) that shows the fields the chosen method needs -- cash shows none, a loan its down/monthly, a lease its down/monthly/lease-end. Non-blocking: the vehicle appears in the list once its required fields (name, next purchase date, price, replacement interval) are set. "Done" closes the editor; it is already saved. When no editor is open the slot rests as the master-detail prompt, which carries the section's only Add. Reads `vehicle_form`, `handle`, `heading`. {% endcomment %}
