{% comment %} The add/edit form for one net-new future vehicle (id=vehicles-form), rendered into the pane's form area beneath the additional-vehicles list. It auto-saves in the background (js-autosave) and always edits a known handle (add mints one), so a new vehicle has a stable identity from the first keystroke. Two groups: the vehicle (what it is, when, how often, and its price) and payment (how it is paid). The payment-method radio drives a switch (inputs.js) that shows the fields the chosen method needs -- cash shows none, a loan its down/monthly, a lease its down/monthly/lease-end. Non-blocking: the vehicle appears in the list once its required fields (name, next purchase date, price, replacement interval) are set. "Done" closes the form; it is already saved. Empty when no form is open. Reads `vehicle_form` and `handle`. {% endcomment %}
