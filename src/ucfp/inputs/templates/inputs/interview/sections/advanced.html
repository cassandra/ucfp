Each sub-pane renders outside the section's Next form (a nested form would be invalid HTML) and persists on its own through its view. The editors come from the section wrapper (`form.economics_form`, `form.taxes_form`, `form.costs_form`, `form.funding_form`, `form.net_worth_form`); each partial is re-rendered by its view on a field error. {% endcomment %}

Net worth calculation

By default, net worth is your assets minus liabilities at face value -- but part of that value isn't yours to keep. Pre-tax retirement accounts and unrealized investment gains carry a latent tax that comes due when you withdraw or sell, so the naive figure can overstate what you'd actually realize. To calculate net worth after that embedded tax, set the rates below and we'll subtract an estimate of it; leave them at 0% (the default) to report gross balances.
