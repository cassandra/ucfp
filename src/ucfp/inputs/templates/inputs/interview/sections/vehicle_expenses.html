{% comment %} The Vehicle Expenses step (Plans flow): the household's cars as a list of vehicles (each added/edited through its own async view, outside the section's Next form) and the shared per-car running costs. Two columns -- the replacement plan (the vehicle list, with the add/edit form opening beneath it) on the left, the running costs on the right. The form lives under the list so it stays next to the Edit it came from and, when the columns stack at narrow widths, appears right after the list rather than below everything. The list and its (initially empty) form area come from the section wrapper (`form.vehicles`, managed by `VehicleFormView` / `VehicleDeleteView`); the running-costs table is `form.vehicle_costs_form` (`VehicleExpensesView`). Each running cost is entered once per car and applies to every car while owned. {% endcomment %}
