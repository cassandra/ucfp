{% comment %} The Vehicle plan step (Plans flow), Profile-driven like the Debt plan. Left column: the household's *current* vehicles as one list -- owned and leased together, each with its plan disposition -- then any *additional* net-new future vehicles; each is edited through its own async view outside the section's Next form, opening beneath its list (a leased row's editor and an owned row's share one form area). Right column: the shared per-car running costs. The disposition rows come from the Vehicles (Profile) section via `form.dispositions` (managed by `VehicleDispositionView` / `LeasedVehicleDispositionView`); the net-new list is `form.vehicles` (`VehicleFormView` / `VehicleDeleteView`); the running-costs table is `form.vehicle_costs_form` (`VehicleExpensesView`). Each running cost is entered once per car and applies to every car while operated. {% endcomment %}
