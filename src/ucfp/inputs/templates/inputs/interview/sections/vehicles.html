{% comment %} The Vehicles pane: one list of the household's current vehicles -- owned and leased together, each with an ownership badge -- edited through its own async view outside the section's Next form (a nested form would be invalid HTML). Adding or editing a vehicle opens the form beneath the list (#current-vehicle-form), where the owned/leased choice drives which fields apply. `form.vehicles` is the combined list, managed by `CurrentVehicleFormView` / `CurrentVehicleDeleteView`. {% endcomment %}

The cars in your driveway, owned or leased -- one list. An owned vehicle with a loan still owed adds that loan to Debts; a leased one is just a name here (its terms are set in the Vehicle plan).
