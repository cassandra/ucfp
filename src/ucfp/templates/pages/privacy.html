Your financial data is yours. Here is how {% settings_value 'SITE_NAME' %} handles it.

  1. We only collect what you give us. Your email address is required to sign in. Everything else — the accounts, income, expenses, and plans you enter — is stored only because you chose to enter it.
  2. We use your data only to provide the service. Your financial information is used to build and show you your own forecast. Nothing else.
  3. No passwords, and email used only when necessary. We never store a password. You sign in with a one-time code we email you, and we use your address only to send those codes and the occasional essential service notice — never marketing.
  4. Your data is encrypted, in transit and at rest. Your connection is protected with HTTPS, and the sensitive figures you enter — your balances and amounts, and the profile and plans that hold them — are encrypted where they are stored, so they stay protected even if the storage or a backup were ever exposed. Our servers build your forecast, so the service can decrypt your figures to do that and nothing else; we have built it so that no one running it has a routine way to browse them — no admin screen, no logs.
  5. We don't track you. No third-party analytics, no advertising trackers, no behavioral profiling.
  6. We don't sell or share your data. Your information stays between you and the service. It is never sold or shared for marketing.
  7. You can delete everything yourself, anytime. From your account page you can permanently delete your account and all your data — immediately, no "contact us and wait". This deletes the data in every household you own, including any you share with others; when you delete your account you can choose to keep a shared household for its other owners instead. You can also delete or leave individual households, or remove records as you work.
  8. You can keep your data on your own machine. {% settings_value 'SITE_NAME' %} is source-available. For the strongest privacy, run your own private instance — your data stays on your computer and never reaches us. Either way, the code is open for anyone to inspect to ensure your data stays yours.
  9. We only use necessary cookies. A session cookie keeps you signed in. There are no tracking cookies.
