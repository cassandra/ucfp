Your financial data is yours. Here is how {% settings_value 'SITE_NAME' %} handles it.

  1. We only collect what you give us. Your email address is required to sign in. Everything else — the accounts, income, expenses, and plans you enter — is stored only because you chose to enter it.
  2. We use your data only to provide the service. Your financial information is used to build and show you your own projections. Nothing else.
  3. No passwords, and email used only when necessary. We never store a password. You sign in with a one-time code we email you, and we use your address only to send those codes and the occasional essential service notice — never marketing.
  4. Your data is encrypted at rest. Your financial information is encrypted where it is stored, so it stays protected even if the underlying storage or backups were ever exposed. Because we build your projections on our servers, our service can process your data to do that — and for nothing else.
  5. We don't track you. No third-party analytics, no advertising trackers, no behavioral profiling.
  6. We don't sell or share your data. Your information stays between you and the service. It is never sold or shared for marketing.
  7. You can delete everything yourself, anytime. Remove individual records as you work, or delete your account and all its data yourself from your account page. When you do, it is permanently erased — no "contact us and wait" required.
  8. You can keep your data on your own machine. {% settings_value 'SITE_NAME' %} is open source. If you would rather no one else host your data, run your own private instance — your data never leaves your computer.
  9. We only use necessary cookies. A session cookie keeps you signed in. There are no tracking cookies.
