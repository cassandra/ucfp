# Frequently Asked Questions

## What can Landfall do today?

Two things, and it aims to do them well:

- **Forecast** your finances year by year into the future, including whether and
  when you can retire. Every figure is fully drillable, down to the account and the
  individual transaction, so you can always see how a number was reached.
- **Explore** "what if" changes: adjust a plan or an assumption and immediately see
  how the forecast shifts.

Landfall is built on a general double-entry financial model, so it is capable of
more over time. But today it is focused on these two things rather than trying to do
everything.

## Is it really free?

Yes. The hosted version at [landfall.cassandrahq.com](https://landfall.cassandrahq.com)
is free to use: no paid tier, no locked features, no ads, no marketing email. The
code is also source-available, so you can run your own instance at no cost.

## Why is it free?

Landfall was built by one person who needed it and would rather share something
useful than lock it away. There is no company behind it that needs your money or
your attention, which is exactly why it doesn't ask for either. No profit motive
means no reason to upsell you, show you ads, or do anything with your data beyond
building your forecast.

## Do I have to give you my email?

No. You can start using Landfall without an account, and your work is saved either
way. The difference is **access, not storage**. Without an email, your data is
reachable only from the browser session you created it in; if you switch devices or
that session is cleared, the data still exists but there's no way to reconnect you to
it. Adding your email links the data to you, so you can sign in from any device and
never lose access. There are no passwords: you sign in with a one-time code we email
you, and we use your address only for those codes and the occasional essential
notice, never marketing.

## Is my data private and safe?

Yes, by design:

- We store **no passwords or credentials**, only the balance summaries and plans
  you type in.
- **No third-party tracking**, no advertising, no behavioral profiling.
- Your figures are **encrypted in transit and at rest**. Our servers can decrypt
  them to build your forecast and nothing else. There is no admin screen or log
  that browses your data.
- You can **delete everything yourself, anytime**, from your account page.

For the full details, see the [Privacy Policy](https://landfall.cassandrahq.com/privacy).
And if you want your data to never leave your own machine at all, self-host (below).

## Should I use the hosted version or self-host?

Either one: they run the same software.

- **Hosted ([landfall.cassandrahq.com](https://landfall.cassandrahq.com))** is the
  easiest: nothing to install, and it's free. Your data lives on our server,
  encrypted.
- **Self-hosting** gives you total control: your data stays entirely on your own
  computer and never reaches us. It takes one command to set up; see the
  [Self-Hosting Guide](SelfHosting.md).

## Can I trust the forecasts?

Trust them, but verify them. No forecasting tool is perfect. Landfall's numbers are
estimates based on the figures and assumptions *you* provide, so they're only as
good as those inputs. What Landfall does give you is **transparency**: every result
is fully drillable, year by year, account by account, transaction by transaction, so
you can check the reasoning for yourself rather than taking a single number on faith.
Before making a decision that matters, satisfy yourself that the numbers are right,
and consider confirming it with a qualified professional. See the
[Terms of Service](https://landfall.cassandrahq.com/terms) for the full disclaimer.

## Is this financial advice?

No. Landfall is a planning and educational tool, not financial, investment, tax, or
legal advice, and its forecasts are not guaranteed. See the
[Terms of Service](https://landfall.cassandrahq.com/terms) for details.

## How does Landfall compare to other tools?

There are other capable tools, and for some people they're the better
choice.  ProjectionLab is very good but its free version is fairly limited,
Boldin is a more established platform with human advisors available but its
free version is similarly restricted, plus it has all the privacy issues and
annoyances you see in so many platforms that have a money-first
ethos. Landfall's focus is being free, private, and no-hassle. Since they
all have a free tier, the easy answer is to try a couple and see what
fits. We keep an honest side-by-side (grid and all) on the [How Landfall
compares](https://landfall.cassandrahq.com/compare) page.
